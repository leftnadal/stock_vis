## 1. Context

The Scientific Philosophy establishes that research begins when reality reveals a limit in current understanding. The Knowledge and Understanding Framework then distinguishes workflow from epistemic object architecture and defines Evidence, Research Claims, Hypothesis roles, Research Knowledge, Understanding, Models, evaluation activities, and admission as distinct concepts.

The existing bootstrap Research Protocol was useful as an initial sequence:

```text
Purpose
→ Research Primitive
→ Research Trigger
→ Research Problem
→ Research Question
→ Hypothesis
→ Research
→ Engineering
→ Evaluation
→ Knowledge
→ Next Trigger
```

Subsequent research found several structural problems with treating every element as a mandatory sequential stage:

- Purpose constrains all research rather than functioning as one-time workflow state.
- Research Primitives structure representation and reasoning rather than functioning as research actions.
- exploratory research may generate Hypotheses instead of beginning with them;
- not every legitimate research undertaking requires Engineering;
- Evaluation should challenge research throughout the lifecycle and also support formal transition decisions;
- an Evaluation result is not identical to a Knowledge admission decision; and
- legitimate research may end unresolved, negative, reframed, or discontinued rather than always producing admitted Knowledge.

The Research Lab therefore developed a Research Methodology v1 backbone that preserves the Trigger → Problem → Question core while introducing proportional Research Design, flexible Investigation, explicit evaluation handoffs, and non-linear lifecycle actions.

## 2. Decision

The Stock_vis Research Lab adopts **Research Methodology v1.0** as the general normative framework for the research lifecycle.

The decision adopts the following commitments.

### 2.1 Treat Purpose and Research Primitives as Governing Context

Purpose constrains the full research lifecycle and is not a sequential stage.

Research Primitives function as semantic structuring lenses rather than workflow states. For v1, the current operational set is Market, Entity, Relationship, State, Transition, and Decision, subject to later reconsideration through legitimate methodology and terminology governance.

### 2.2 Preserve Trigger → Problem → Question as the Core Research Origin

A research undertaking must remain traceable to a Research Trigger arising from an unresolved condition in Reality. The Trigger is framed as a Research Problem, and the specific object of investigation is formulated as a Research Question.

### 2.3 Add Research Design as an Explicit Methodological Function

Research Question is followed by a proportional Research Design or Strategy that makes material scope, evidence strategy, alternatives, assumptions, methods, and intended evaluation visible before they become hidden features of results.

### 2.4 Make Hypothesis Conditional Rather Than Universal

A Hypothesis is an explicit research role for an investigable Research Claim. It is not mandatory for every research undertaking.

Exploratory research may generate Claims and Hypotheses during Investigation. Confirmatory work requires stronger separation between discovery and independent confirmation when the intended inference materially depends on that distinction.

### 2.5 Define Investigation as a Flexible Activity Space

Investigation may include evidence acquisition, analysis, comparison, modeling, engineering, experiment, simulation, synthesis, and other methods as appropriate to the Question and Design.

Engineering is conditional rather than a mandatory stage.

### 2.6 Separate Evaluation From Research Lifecycle Action

Evaluation Methodology provides epistemic assessment and challenge. Research Methodology owns the research lifecycle and determines what happens next in response to a structured assessment.

Evaluation therefore does not automatically promote a candidate result into Knowledge.

Every Research Knowledge admission requires an admission-purpose structured evaluation, with rigor proportional to epistemic consequence.

### 2.7 Treat Knowledge Admission as One Possible Outcome

Research may continue, revise, reframe, restrict, remain unresolved, stop, or admit a bounded result as Research Knowledge.

Research Knowledge is not the mandatory terminal state of every undertaking.

### 2.8 Require Knowledge-to-Understanding Impact Checking Rather Than Automatic Promotion

Admission or revision of Knowledge may trigger assessment of dependent Understandings, Models, or further research, but it does not automatically create or revise an Understanding.

Material change propagates review obligations rather than predetermined downstream conclusions.

### 2.9 Use Proportional Research Rigor

Research Design, documentation, and challenge should be proportional to epistemic consequence, uncertainty, downstream reuse, novelty, complexity, reversibility, and consequences of being wrong.

Urgency or exploratory status may justify provisional use or lighter process, but it does not redefine epistemic quality.

### 2.10 Distinguish Re-evaluation and Reconsideration Triggers

Research Methodology uses the Re-evaluation Trigger and Reconsideration Trigger definitions established by Evaluation Methodology.

A Re-evaluation Trigger may reopen assessment of a particular epistemic object or evaluation. A Reconsideration Trigger may reopen an existing methodology, architecture, assumption, or consequential decision to determine whether it should be retained, modified, restricted, or replaced.

Neither trigger predetermines the outcome.

### 2.11 Coordinate the Initial v1 Adoption

Research Methodology v1.0 and Evaluation Methodology v1.0 form one coordinated initial adoption set because each defines one side of the research–evaluation handoff.

They should become effective together for their initial v1 adoption. After that initial adoption, either methodology may evolve through its own revision lifecycle when dependency review confirms continued consistency with the other and with upstream authorities.

## 3. Alternatives Considered

### 3.1 Preserve the Existing Linear Protocol With Optional Markers

The Research Lab could retain the original sequence and mark Hypothesis, Engineering, or Knowledge as optional.

This was not selected because the apparent simplicity would conceal a branching and iterative lifecycle behind a nominally linear pipeline. It would also leave Evaluation positioned too strongly as a terminal stage.

### 3.2 Require a Hypothesis for Every Research Undertaking

This would simplify research records and agent workflow.

It was not selected because exploratory research may legitimately begin with a broad unresolved condition and generate testable Claims only after initial Investigation. Mandatory Hypotheses would create pressure for premature closure and post-hoc confirmation.

### 3.3 Require Engineering Before Evaluation

This would fit artifact- and model-building research but would incorrectly impose implementation work on literature, conceptual, comparative, market-structure, and other research that can produce legitimate outputs without Engineering.

### 3.4 Let Evaluation Own Admission and Next Actions

Evaluation could produce PASS/FAIL and directly determine Knowledge admission or rejection.

This was not selected because assessment and lifecycle action are distinct responsibilities. Collapsing them would turn Evaluation into an automatic epistemic promotion mechanism and blur the boundary with Research Methodology.

### 3.5 Require Every Research Undertaking to Produce Knowledge

This was not selected because unresolved, null, negative, failed, reframed, or gap-producing research may be scientifically valuable without producing an admitted Knowledge item.

## 4. Rationale

The adopted methodology preserves the strongest part of the original Research Protocol while making its implicit branching and iteration explicit.

The Research Lab retains the distinctive commitment that research begins from unresolved Reality rather than from a Question alone. It also preserves epistemic discipline by separating research roles, investigation activities, evaluation, lifecycle decisions, and Knowledge admission.

The methodology is intentionally proportional and implementation-neutral. It creates enough structure to support future agent workflows and automation without allowing a workflow engine, state machine, or database schema to determine research meaning prematurely.

## 5. Consequences

### 5.1 Binding Implications

Adoption of Research Methodology v1.0 establishes that:

- Purpose and Research Primitives must not be represented as mandatory sequential workflow states;
- formal research must remain traceable from Trigger to Problem to Question;
- Research Design is a general methodological function, with rigor proportional to consequence;
- Hypothesis and Engineering are conditional rather than universally mandatory;
- exploratory discovery and confirmation must remain distinguishable when epistemically material;
- Evaluation may operate throughout the lifecycle and at formal checkpoints;
- every Research Knowledge admission requires an applicable admission-purpose evaluation;
- Evaluation results do not automatically determine Knowledge admission;
- Research Knowledge is one possible research outcome rather than a mandatory terminal state; and
- material Knowledge change may trigger Understanding impact review and further research rather than automatic downstream revision.

### 5.2 Expected Benefits

The methodology is expected to provide:

- better alignment between research workflow and the Scientific Philosophy;
- reduced pressure to force exploratory work into premature hypotheses;
- less unnecessary Engineering and process bureaucracy;
- clearer handoffs between Research and Evaluation Methodology;
- safer Knowledge admission;
- better handling of unresolved or negative research outcomes; and
- a more evolvable basis for future sub-agent research automation.

### 5.3 Required Follow-on Work

Later work may include:

- operational Research Design templates;
- research-lifecycle vocabulary refinement;
- concrete research-record requirements;
- agent orchestration and escalation rules;
- implementation of impact propagation and triggers; and
- empirical calibration of proportional research burden.

## 6. Non-Decisions

This decision does not adopt:

- a universal Research Design template;
- an exhaustive research-type taxonomy;
- an exhaustive method taxonomy;
- exact admission thresholds;
- exact lifecycle-state vocabulary;
- agent-level admission or escalation authority;
- a workflow-engine or state-machine implementation;
- a database or ontology schema;
- numerical credibility scales; or
- investment decision rules.
