# Stock_vis Research Lab

This directory is the official knowledge library for Stock_vis research, governance, methodology, evaluation, and model documentation.

The structure and contents of this library will evolve through discussion, explicit approval, and versioned updates.

## Operational Research Candidates

Potential research paths that are intentionally retained but are not yet active Research Cases are tracked in the [Research Candidate Registry](research_candidate_registry.md) ([한국어 companion](research_candidate_registry_ko.md)).

Registry inclusion does not mean Research Knowledge admission, Research Case approval, priority assignment, resource commitment, or scheduled execution.
