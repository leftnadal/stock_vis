# Methodology

This directory contains the Research Lab's operational methodology, including semantic governance, epistemic architecture, the research lifecycle used to move from unresolved reality toward evaluated Research Knowledge and improved Understanding, and implementation-neutral operational record semantics.

Research Methodology defines how research undertakings are framed and progressed. Evaluation Methodology is maintained separately under `02_evaluation` and provides the epistemic assessment system used throughout and after research. Operational Record Specification defines how material research state, epistemic objects, evaluations, Evidence references, and their relationships are persistently separated and made reconstructable without fixing a physical storage implementation.
