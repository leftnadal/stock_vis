# Methodology

This directory contains the Research Lab's operational methodology, including semantic governance, epistemic architecture, and the research lifecycle used to move from unresolved reality toward evaluated Research Knowledge and improved Understanding.

Research Methodology defines how research undertakings are framed and progressed. Evaluation Methodology is maintained separately under `02_evaluation` and provides the epistemic assessment system used throughout and after research.
