# Stock_vis Design Lab

This directory is the official knowledge and operating library for the Stock_vis Design Lab.

The Design Lab exists to study and improve how Stock_vis supports better investment judgment in service of the broader Stock_vis purpose of better investment decisions.

## Authority

- Research concepts and Research Knowledge are governed by [`research_lab/`](../research_lab/).
- Design Lab purpose, operating knowledge, Design Knowledge, workstreams, and Design decisions are governed within `design_lab/` according to their approved authority.
- Design Lab documents must not silently redefine Research Lab concepts.

## Growth Model

The Design Lab starts with the minimum structure required to operate. New workstream, knowledge, decision, evaluation, terminology, or design-system structures are added only when real work creates a recurring need for them.

The structure itself is expected to evolve through use, observation, explicit review when consequential, and versioned updates.
