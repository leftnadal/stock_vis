# Stock_vis Design Lab Foundation

**Owner:** Stock_vis Design Lab

## 1. Purpose

Stock_vis ultimately exists to support:

> **Better Investment Decisions**

The current working direct purpose of the Design Lab is:

> **Better Investment Judgment**

The Design Lab studies how users can better perceive, explore, compare, understand, and use relevant information and Research Knowledge when forming or revising investment judgment.

The Design Lab does not exist to make investment decisions on behalf of the user.

This direct purpose remains a working foundation until explicitly elevated through a later approved Design decision.

## 2. Research–Design Boundary

The Research Lab is the authority for Research Knowledge and for the official meaning of Research concepts.

The Design Lab does not redefine Research concepts for interface convenience. When Design work requires a Research concept, the relevant authority in `research_lab/` should be used.

The Design Lab instead studies the downstream design question:

> How should Research Knowledge and other relevant information be represented and experienced so that users can form better investment judgment?

Research Definition and Design Interpretation must remain distinguishable.

If Design work reveals a gap, conflict, or missing Research concept, it should be treated as a Research Trigger Candidate or cross-Lab issue rather than silently resolved through a new Design-side definition.

## 3. Working Design Philosophy

The following statements are the current working philosophical direction. They are intentionally concise and remain open to revision through real Design work.

### 3.1 Reveal what matters for judgment

Design should help users distinguish what is important for investment judgment rather than merely presenting what is available or visually dramatic.

Large changes, prominent headlines, and high-volume information are not automatically the most judgment-relevant information.

### 3.2 Reduce complexity without erasing necessary structure

Design should make complex reality more usable while preserving relationships, conditions, conflicting evidence, uncertainty, and other structure that may materially affect judgment.

Simplicity must not create false certainty or misleading compression.

### 3.3 Strengthen user judgment without replacing it

Design may actively organize, interpret, compare, explain, and surface relevant evidence, but it should preserve the user’s ability to form and revise judgment.

Decision support must not silently become decision prescription.

## 4. Working Nature of the Foundation

This document is deliberately minimal.

Detailed user models, judgment journeys, information architecture, product surfaces, interaction patterns, terminology, evaluation methods, and Design Knowledge should not be promoted into Foundation merely because they are currently useful.

They should be explored in workstreams and formalized only when repeated use, evidence, or long-term dependency justifies stronger status.
