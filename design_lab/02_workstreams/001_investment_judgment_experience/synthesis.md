**Status:** Working / Not Yet Synthesized

## Purpose

This document will contain the integrated Design Lab Lead synthesis after the workstream has completed sufficient exploration, critique, scenario stress-testing, and Research consistency review.

It must not be filled by simply restating the starting hypotheses.

## Required Synthesis Structure

When sufficient evidence exists, the synthesis should state:

### 1. Problem Framing

- recommended formulation of the core investment judgment problem;
- major rejected or narrowed framings;
- evidence and limitations.

### 2. Recommended Judgment Model

- what investment judgment appears to consist of;
- what should remain user-visible versus system-side;
- how uncertainty and conviction should be treated;
- key alternatives and why they were not preferred.

### 3. Recommended Judgment Journey / Interaction Logic

- whether a common journey or loop is justified;
- where non-linearity is required;
- differences among initial formation, updating, and comparative judgment.

### 4. Recommended Information Model

- the minimum information structures needed to support better judgment;
- relationships among change, context, evidence, significance, judgment, and conviction;
- concepts that should be merged, split, or rejected.

### 5. Logical Experience Architecture

- whether a model such as `Orient → Understand → Judge` survives testing;
- whether it should guide user experience, internal reasoning, or both;
- implications for later Product Surface / IA exploration without prematurely fixing navigation.

### 6. Failure Modes and Reversal Conditions

- scenarios where the recommended model performs poorly;
- assumptions requiring real user validation;
- signals that should trigger revision.

### 7. Design Knowledge Candidates

Identify findings that may deserve promotion beyond this workstream. Promotion must follow `knowledge_lifecycle.md` and is not automatic.

### 8. Research Trigger Candidates

Identify any unresolved question requiring Research Lab authority or new Research work.

### 9. Design Lab Evolution Findings

Record only recurring or structural operating issues worth changing in the Design Lab itself.

### 10. Decision Package

When the workstream reaches a material design boundary, present:

- Recommended Structure;
- Recommendation Strength;
- Why;
- Main Alternative(s);
- Key Trade-offs;
- Failure / Reversal Conditions;
- Batch Consensus items;
- CEO Critical Decision(s), limited to genuinely consequential decisions;
- Deferred / AI-owned items.

If there is no CEO Critical Decision, state that explicitly.
